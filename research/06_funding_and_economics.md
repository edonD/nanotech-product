# Funding Landscape & Health Economics for Rapid AST

## Sepsis Economics

### Per-Patient Cost Data
- **Mean sepsis patient cost**: $32,421 (median across studies, 2014-adjusted)
- **POA sepsis**: $18,023 per patient
- **Non-POA (hospital-acquired) sepsis**: $51,022 per patient
- **ICU cost**: $27,461 median mean per patient

### Length of Stay
- **Sepsis ICU stay**: 5.2-10.1 days (depending on severity)
- **Total hospital stay**: 7.7-17.6 days
- **Sepsis patients stay 75% longer** than other hospitalized patients

### National Burden
- **$24 billion/year** — sepsis is the most expensive condition in the US healthcare system
- Grew by $3.4B in recent years due to increasing complexity and resistance

### RAPIDE Value Proposition
- If RAPIDE reduces inappropriate empiric therapy from 48h+ to <1h:
  - Estimated **1.75-6 fewer hospital days** per sepsis patient
  - At $2,000-4,000/day ICU cost → **$3,500-$24,000 savings per patient**
  - Even conservative 1-day reduction across 1M US sepsis patients = **$2-4 billion/year savings**
- Per-test cost of $40-50 generates **massive ROI** for hospitals

## Non-Dilutive Funding Sources

### CARB-X (Key Opportunity)
- **Combating Antibiotic-Resistant Bacteria Biopharmaceutical Accelerator**
- BARDA + Wellcome Trust + governments = **$370M+ in committed funding**
- Explicitly funds diagnostics for AMR
- 2025 focus: "diagnosis of acute infections in 60 minutes or less" — **directly aligns with RAPIDE**
- Previously funded SpeeDx for rapid POC diagnostic
- 2026 funding round announced — RAPIDE should apply
- Typical awards: $1-5M for early-stage diagnostics

### BARDA Direct
- **$2.7 billion invested** in antimicrobial products since 2010
- Led to FDA 510(k) clearance of **10 diagnostics**
- DRIVe program specifically targets innovative diagnostics
- Partnership model: milestone-based funding

### NIH/NIAID
- SBIR/STTR grants ($150K Phase I, $1M Phase II)
- R01 grants for technology validation
- Point-of-care diagnostics is a priority area

### AMR Action Fund
- $1B fund launched by pharma companies (2020)
- Focused on antibiotics but diagnostics are synergistic

### Wellcome Trust
- Independent funder of AMR research
- Part of CARB-X consortium
- Targets diagnostics for low-resource settings

### EU Horizon Europe
- AMR is a priority for EU research funding
- IMI/IHI partnerships fund diagnostic development
