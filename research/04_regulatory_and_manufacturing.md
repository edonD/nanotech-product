# Regulatory Landscape & Manufacturing Strategy

## FDA Breakthrough Device Designations for AMR Diagnostics

The FDA is actively encouraging rapid AMR diagnostics through its Breakthrough Device program:

### Recent Designations (2025-2026)
- **bioMérieux SPECIFIC REVEAL®**: Rapid AST system — FDA Breakthrough Device Designation
- **NG Biotech NG-TEST® Candida auris**: First lateral flow immunoassay for C. auris (Feb 2026)
- **NG Biotech NG-TEST® Acineto-5®**: Rapid carbapenemase detection in Acinetobacter (Feb 2026)
- **Inbiome Molecular Culture ID**: Molecular diagnostics — Breakthrough Device Designation, US market entry early 2026

### Implications for RAPIDE
- FDA is clearly prioritizing rapid AMR diagnostics
- Breakthrough Device Designation provides faster regulatory pathway
- Pre-submission meetings with FDA are available for breakthrough candidates
- Strong precedent for rapid AST devices receiving expedited review

## Manufacturing Cost Analysis for Disposable Microfluidic Chips

### Material Options for Production Scale

| Material | Unit Cost (High Volume) | Tooling Cost | Best For |
|----------|----------------------|--------------|----------|
| Silicon/Glass (MEMS) | $5-15/chip | $50K-200K masks | Prototype, integrated sensors |
| PDMS (soft lithography) | $2-5/chip | $5K-20K molds | Research, low volume |
| PMMA/PC (injection molding) | $0.50-2/chip | $30K-100K mold | Mass production, disposable |
| COC (hot embossing) | $1-3/chip | $20K-80K mold | Optical clarity, disposable |

### Hybrid Approach for RAPIDE
1. **Sensor substrate** (silicon/glass): MEMS-fabricated impedance electrodes — reusable component on reader OR disposable wafer-level fabricated sensor layer
2. **Microfluidic layer** (injection-molded thermoplastic): DLD arrays, channels, chambers — disposable
3. **Reagent storage** (foil-sealed reservoirs): Standard pharmaceutical lyophilization

### Production Cost Projections

**At 10K chips/year (early stage):**
- Sensor substrate: ~$8/chip (small batch MEMS)
- Microfluidic layer: ~$5/chip (CNC or 3D printed)
- Reagents + packaging: ~$7/chip
- **Total BOM: ~$20/chip** → Sell at $45-50

**At 100K chips/year (growth stage):**
- Sensor substrate: ~$3/chip (wafer-level batch processing)
- Microfluidic layer: ~$1.50/chip (injection molding)
- Reagents + packaging: ~$5/chip
- **Total BOM: ~$10/chip** → Sell at $35-40

**At 1M chips/year (scale):**
- Sensor substrate: ~$1.50/chip (high-volume MEMS foundry)
- Microfluidic layer: ~$0.50/chip
- Reagents + packaging: ~$3/chip
- **Total BOM: ~$5/chip** → Sell at $30-35, ~80% gross margin

### Key Manufacturing Partners
- **MEMS foundries**: STMicroelectronics, X-FAB, Silex Microsystems
- **Microfluidics**: Dolomite, Micralyne, uFluidix
- **Contract manufacturing**: Jabil, Flex, Benchmark Electronics

## Regulatory Strategy Update

### Phase 1: Research Use Only (RUO) — Year 1-2
- No FDA clearance needed
- Sell to academic medical centers and research hospitals
- Build clinical evidence, refine product-market fit

### Phase 2: FDA Breakthrough Device Pathway — Year 2-3
- Apply for Breakthrough Device Designation (strong precedent)
- Pre-submission meeting with FDA CDRH
- Target De Novo classification or 510(k) with predicate (Accelerate Pheno, ASTar)
- Initial indication: UTI (simplest regulatory path)

### Phase 3: Expanded Clearance — Year 3-5
- Bloodstream infections (most impactful, harder regulatory)
- CE marking (EU MDR) in parallel
- WHO prequalification for global health markets
